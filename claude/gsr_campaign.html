The Mahindra Scorpio Getaway

We're acquiring a second-hand Mahindra Scorpio Getaway—a 4x4 workhorse purpose-built for Nepal's mountain terrain. It features strong diesel engine performance, 4x4 capability, high ground clearance, and reliable load-carrying capacity.

⚙️

Diesel Power

Optimised for high-altitude travel and fuel efficiency across Nepal's challenging geography.

🏔️

All-Terrain 4x4

Off-road and rural terrain access with high ground clearance for rough, unpaved roads.

📦

Cargo Ready

Reliable load-carrying capacity for ministry equipment, educational resources, and humanitarian supplies.

Serving Remote Communities

The vehicle will support the Crowns Worship Ministry and partner with IAHC Nepal to provide educational resources to underprivileged children, support families in crisis, and enable worship training across Nepal's most isolated regions. It will be registered in the charity's name and dedicated to tangible, lasting impact.

Every Pound Moves Mountains

Your donation directly funds a vehicle that will serve vulnerable families, equip young leaders, strengthen rural churches, and deliver education to communities most in need. This isn't just about transport—it's about access, impact, and transformation.
